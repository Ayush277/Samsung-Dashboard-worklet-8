What This Teaches Us

This example demonstrates that loan performance prediction is complex and that borrowers with risk indicators can still perform well. It highlights the importance of comprehensive analysis rather than single-factor decisions, and shows why machine learning models that consider multiple variables simultaneously can be more effective than simple rule-based systems.
