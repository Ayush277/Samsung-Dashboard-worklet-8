🎯 System Overview

The Loan Risk Predictor is an AI-powered system that analyzes loan applications and predicts the probability of default using advanced machine learning techniques trained on over 116,000 historical loan records.

🎯 Purpose

  • ✅ Predict loan default probability
  • ✅ Assess borrower risk levels
  • ✅ Provide business recommendations
  • ✅ Support lending decisions

🎨 Key Features

  • ✅ Comprehensive input validation
  • ✅ Real-time risk analysis
  • ✅ Risk driver identification
  • ✅ Interactive tutorial system

👥 Target Users

  • ✅ Loan officers
  • ✅ Risk managers
  • ✅ Credit analysts
  • ✅ Financial institutions

📝 Input Form Guide

The system requires comprehensive information organized into three main categories:

🏠 Loan Information (8 fields)

  • 💰 Interest Rate (%)
  • 💰 Principal Balance ($)
  • 📅 Loan Term (months)
  • 📈 Loan-to-Value Ratio (%)
  • 📍 Source Channel (X/Y/Z)
  • 🎯 Loan Purpose (A23/B12/C86)
  • 👥 Number of Borrowers
  • 🛡️ Insurance Coverage (%)

👤 Borrower Profile (9 fields)

  • 📊 Credit Score (300-850)
  • 💵 Annual Income ($)
  • 🎂 Age (18-100)
  • 🎓 Education Level
  • 💍 Marital Status
  • ⚧️ Gender
  • 💼 Employment Status
  • 👶 Number of Dependents
  • 📈 Debt-to-Income Ratio

💳 Payment History (5 fields)

  • ✅ Total On-Time Payments
  • ⏰ Total Late Payments
  • 📊 Average Payment Delay
  • ⚠️ Current Days Past Due
  • 👥 Co-borrower Credit Score

💡 Input Tips

  • Required Fields (*): Must be completed for accurate prediction
  • Optional Fields: Improve prediction accuracy when provided
  • Validation: System checks ranges and formats automatically
  • Missing Data: Filled with portfolio medians from training data

📏 Risk Criteria & Scoring System

Risk Factor Weight Low Risk Indicators High Risk Indicators
Payment History Very High No late payments, current on all accounts Multiple late payments, currently delinquent
Credit Score High Score ≥ 720 (excellent credit) Score < 620 (poor credit)
Debt-to-Income Ratio High DTI < 30% (manageable debt load) DTI > 40% (high debt burden)
Annual Income Moderate Stable, sufficient income for loan Very low income relative to loan amount
Employment Status Moderate Steady employment, stable job Unemployed, frequent job changes
Loan Characteristics Low Reasonable LTV, standard terms High LTV, aggressive terms

📐 Risk Score Calculation Formula:

Risk Score = Σ (Feature_i × Weight_i × Z_score_i)
where Z_score = (User_Value - Portfolio_Median) / Standard_Deviation

🎯 Example 1: High Risk Profile (Tutorial Example)

  • 💵 Income: $1,951/year (very low!)
  • 📊 Credit Score: 690

📊 Model Output

Moderate Risk (43.6%)

  • 🛡️ Confidence: 12.8%

  • 💡 Recommendation: Conditional approval
🎓 Learning Point: Despite multiple risk indicators (low income, young age, payment delays), this loan was historically classified as "On-Time" (mx=0), demonstrating the complexity of loan performance prediction.
